# Tiny Wars ⚔️

## Overview

Tiny Wars is a 2D real-time strategy (RTS) mobile game developed in Unity 6 for Android and iOS. Inspired by classic RTS gameplay, the game combines pixel-art visuals, strategic combat, resource management, and intelligent AI systems into a lightweight and accessible mobile experience.

Players can build structures, gather resources, train armies, and command units in real-time battles across tile-based maps.

## Features

- **Tilemap-Based World**: Grid-based RTS maps built using Unity Tilemaps.
- **Building Placement System**: Construct and manage buildings dynamically during gameplay.
- **A* Pathfinding**: Intelligent unit navigation and obstacle avoidance.
- **AI Systems**: Enemy and player AI behaviors with autonomous combat logic.
- **Real-Time Combat**: Fast-paced battles with animated units and attack systems.
- **Resource Economy**: Gather and manage gold and lumber resources.
- **Enemy Wave System**: Dynamic enemy spawning and scaling difficulty.
- **Unit Training & Stances**: Train units and control offensive or defensive formations.
- **LDO Mechanics**: Strategic Location, Deployment, and Objective-based gameplay.

## Gameplay

Players expand their base, gather resources, deploy units, and defend against enemy attacks while completing strategic objectives. Battles happen in real time, requiring tactical positioning, efficient economy management, and smart unit deployment.
