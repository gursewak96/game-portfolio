# Carrot Clicker 🥕

## Overview

Carrot Clicker is a fun and addictive idle clicker game where players tap on carrots to earn points and resources. The game features satisfying click feedback, passive income mechanics, and smooth animations powered by Timeline and LeanTween. Watch your carrot farm grow as you progress!

## Features

- **Click-based Gameplay**: Tap carrots to earn points and resources
- **Idle Mechanics**: Earn rewards passively over time without active playing
- **Offline Earning**: Continue earning rewards even when the game is closed
- **Daily Rewards**: Collect bonus rewards every day you log in with streak tracking
- **Timeline Integration**: Polished animations and visual effects
- **LeanTween Animations**: Smooth and efficient tweening effects
- **Progress Saving**: Game state persistence across sessions
- **Custom UI**: Intuitive and responsive user interface
- **Upgrades System**: Purchase upgrades to increase click value and idle income

## Gameplay

Click on the carrot to earn points! Use your earnings to purchase upgrades that increase your click value and passive income. Let the game run passively to earn idle rewards, or come back daily for bonus rewards. The more you upgrade, the faster your carrot farm grows!

## Game Mechanics

### Click System
- Each click on the carrot earns a base amount of points
- Click multipliers can be earned through upgrades

### Idle System
- Passive income is generated based on owned upgrades
- Players earn rewards even when the game is closed

### Offline Earning
- Game tracks time elapsed while the app is closed
- Players earn accumulated rewards proportional to offline time
- Offline earnings are added upon returning to the game

### Daily Rewards
- Exclusive rewards available once per day
- Login streak tracking for bonus multipliers
- Encourage daily engagement and player retention

### Upgrades
- Players can purchase upgrades to increase click value
- Permanent upgrades enhance idle income
- Multipliers accelerate progression

## Technologies Used

- **Engine**: Unity 2021 LTS+
- **Language**: C#
- **Animation**: Unity Timeline, LeanTween
- **UI Framework**: Unity UI/uGUI
- **Text Rendering**: TextMesh Pro
