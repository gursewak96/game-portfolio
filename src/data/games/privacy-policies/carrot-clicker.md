# Privacy Policy for Carrot Clicker

**Last Updated: June 24, 2026**

## Introduction

Carrot Clicker ("the Application") is an independent project developed to provide an engaging user experience. We are committed to protecting your privacy and ensuring you have a positive experience on our application.

This Privacy Policy explains our practices regarding data collection, usage, storage, and protection. Please read this policy carefully to understand our views and practices regarding your personal data and how we treat it.

## 1. No Data Collection Policy

Carrot Clicker does **not actively collect personal information** about you. We do not require login credentials, email addresses, or personal identifiers to use the Application.

### What We Don't Do
- We do not collect or transmit personal information about you
- We do not track your identity
- We do not use analytics services that identify users
- We do not share information with third parties
- We do not sell or monetize user data

## 2. Local Data Storage

Any data generated by your use of Carrot Clicker (including but not limited to game progress, settings, preferences, and scores) is stored **exclusively on your device** and is not transmitted to external servers.

### Types of Local Data
- Game progress and save files
- User preferences and settings
- Graphics and audio configuration
- Gameplay statistics and scores
- Input preferences

All this data remains under your control on your local device.

## 3. No External Communication

Carrot Clicker does not communicate with external servers or services to collect, store, or process your data. Your device operates independently, and your data remains entirely on your machine.

## 4. No Third-Party Services

This Application does not integrate with third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking services. We do not use:
- Analytics platforms (e.g., Google Analytics)
- Advertising networks
- Social media tracking pixels
- Cloud storage services for user data
- External APIs that collect personal information

## 5. Cookies and Local Storage

While we do not use tracking cookies, the Application may use local storage mechanisms (such as PlayerPrefs in Unity) to store your preferences and game state. These are stored entirely on your device.

## 6. Security

Since all data is stored locally on your device, the security of your data is primarily dependent on your device's security practices. We recommend:
- Keeping your device's operating system updated
- Using strong passwords and security measures
- Regularly backing up your save files if desired
- Using antivirus and anti-malware software

## 7. User Data Deletion

Since all data is stored locally on your device, you have full control over it. To delete all data:
1. Delete the game application
2. Manually delete any associated local files or save data

We do not maintain any copies of your data on our servers.

## 8. Age Restrictions

Carrot Ticker is suitable for all ages. We do not knowingly collect any information from children, as we do not collect information from anyone.

## 9. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our practices or for other operational, legal, or regulatory reasons. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated "Last Updated" date. Continued use of the Application after such modifications constitutes your acceptance of the updated Privacy Policy.

## 10. Contact Us

If you have any questions, concerns, or requests regarding this Privacy Policy or our privacy practices, please contact us through the project repository or associated documentation.

## 11. Disclaimer

This Application is provided "as is" without any warranties. We are not responsible for any data loss or damage that may occur on your device. We recommend regular backups of your important save files.

## 12. Compliance

This Privacy Policy is designed to comply with:
- GDPR (General Data Protection Regulation)
- CCPA (California Consumer Privacy Act)
- Other applicable privacy regulations

Since we do not collect personal data, many requirements of these regulations do not apply; however, this policy ensures full transparency regarding our minimal data handling practices.
